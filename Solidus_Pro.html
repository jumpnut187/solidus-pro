Coupled thermal solver

Temperature-dependent conductivity, heat capacity and density. Enthalpy latent heat and Scheil fraction solid. Mold interfaces with contact conductance, and chills modeled as finite heat sinks.

Defect prediction

Shrinkage, porosity, hot spots, Niyama, hot tear, sand burn-in and traced feeding paths. Each one is a field you can inspect voxel by voxel.

Parametric modeling

Sketch and extrude risers, chills and pads right in the browser. "Up To Body" end conditions make each one sit exactly on a curved casting surface.

Native hardware speed

JIT-compiled loops on physical cores, Metal on Apple Silicon, plus CUDA and OpenCL. Adaptive octree voxels, and a disk-backed mode for models above 10 million voxels.

Learned surrogate models

Fast solidification estimates from a 3D U-Net trained on your own simulation runs. It runs on the NPU in the machine: the Apple Neural Engine, Qualcomm, Intel or AMD NPUs, or an NVIDIA GPU.

Results engineers can read

Fields painted on the CAD surface, threshold envelopes, section cuts, animated fraction liquid, reports in HTML, PDF and Markdown, and MP4/GIF export.

AI acceleration

Runs on the NPU
already in the machine.

The deep surrogate exports to Core ML and ONNX. On a Mac it runs on the Apple Neural Engine. On a Windows or Linux machine it runs through ONNX Runtime on a Qualcomm, Intel or AMD NPU, an NVIDIA GPU, or the CPU. On those machines Solidus Pro doesn't assume the NPU is fastest: it checks the result against the reference model, times both, and keeps the faster one.

Parametric modeling

Risers that fit
the casting exactly.

Feeders and chills are built from editable sketch and extrude features. Change a diameter later and the body rebuilds against the casting.

  • 1
    Sketch on a planeDatum planes, faces of the casting, or offset reference planes.
  • 2
    Extrude Up To BodyThe extrude runs until it meets the casting.
  • 3
    Conforming contactThe contact face takes the casting's curved shape, so the body is watertight and ready to simulate.
