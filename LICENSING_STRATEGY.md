## 2. What the AGPL requires

The AGPL is GPLv3 plus a network clause:

- If you **distribute** the software or a modified version, you must provide
  the complete corresponding source under AGPL-3.0-or-later, keep the license
  notices, and state what you changed.
- If you **run a modified version as a network service**, users interacting
  with it must be offered the corresponding source. This closes the SaaS
  loophole that plain GPLv3 leaves open.
- You may not add further restrictions, sublicense the code, or use patent
  claims to restrict other users.

Running an unmodified copy internally or privately is not distribution and
carries no source-release obligation.

Contributions are accepted under the same license (**inbound = outbound**).
By submitting a pull request you agree that your contribution is licensed
under AGPL-3.0-or-later. You keep your copyright; no CLA is required and no

Bundled or linked dependencies keep their own licenses. See
